4. Farming Calculations and Financial Estimates

Aqua Manager provides tools to calculate farming investments, expenses, estimated harvest revenue, and potential profit or loss based on information entered by users.

Total Investment and Expenses
Total Investment = Seed Cost + Feed Cost + Labour Cost + Medicines + Electricity + Other Expenses.

Estimated Harvest Revenue
Estimated Revenue = Expected Harvest Quantity × Expected Selling Price per Unit.

Estimated Profit or Loss
Estimated Profit or Loss = Estimated Revenue − Total Investment and Expenses.

These calculations are estimates based on user-provided information. Actual results may vary because of mortality, disease, weather, water quality, feed efficiency, market prices, and other factors.

Aqua Manager does not guarantee farming profits, harvest quantities, investment returns, or financial outcomes. Users are responsible for verifying calculations and making their own farming and investment decisions.
